About 1 minute 15. Two official files, 2020-2024 and the current file, same 13 columns, consecutive periods, so we append them. 754,413 records. We aggregate to one row per division per day: 14,748 division-days, median 53, never zero. The table describes the target. Mean and median agree, so a typical day is symmetric. The tails are not: Division 1 had 384 interventions on the ice-storm day, 13 times its median. That is why we use MAE as the primary metric and why we look at storm days separately.
