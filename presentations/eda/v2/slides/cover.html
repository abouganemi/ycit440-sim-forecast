YCIT 440 capstone, EDA milestone

What the SIM open data can support

Exploratory analysis for a one-day-ahead forecast of published interventions in Divisions 1-6

Team 4: Cristal Cortez, Isaac Abouganem, Peining Huang, Karl Moubayed, Langyue Sun
October 15, 2026

About 30 seconds. Introduce the team and the milestone. One sentence on the project: we want to forecast, one day ahead, how many interventions the Montréal fire service (SIM) will record in each of its six operational divisions. This talk covers what we checked in the data, what we found, and what it means for the model.
