Next steps, risks and assumptions

Next steps

  1. Baseline and rolling-origin validation harness that enforces the D−3 cutoff
  2. Feature pipeline: recent levels, calendar, holidays
  3. Single models: LightGBM, XGBoost, CatBoost and a negative-binomial GLM
  4. Ensemble, compared with both baselines using paired bootstrap intervals

Risks and assumptions

  • The late-2025 first-responder drop has no documented cause; a dispatch-protocol change is only a hypothesis
  • The D−3 cutoff is simulated: the portal keeps no history of publication dates
  • The open data cover about 94% of SIM activity
  • Weather drives the surges but is outside the agreed design
  • Usefulness to SIM planners is unverified without access to SIM
