Backup, for questions about the baselines. Over four validation years the two baselines tie: 8.48 for the 13-week same-weekday mean against 8.49 for the plain 28-day mean, and each wins two divisions. The 2024-only preview, where the plain mean looked better, does not hold over 2021 to 2024. The 13-week window was chosen on these years, before any test data was used. Negative bias means slight under-forecasting; it comes mostly from anomaly days and from skewed counts, where the median sits below the mean.
