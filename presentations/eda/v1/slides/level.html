About 1 minute 15. First pattern: the level is not stable, and the breaks come from first-responder calls, the blue band. In spring 2020 they almost disappear for three months (COVID). From late December 2025 they fall 25 to 30% in Divisions 1 to 5, while all other groups hold or grow, and Division 6 grows. We do not know why; a change in dispatch protocol is a hypothesis, not a finding. For the model: 2020 is only lag history, structure is learned on 2021 to 2024, and the late-2025 break falls inside our test period, so it is a real drift test.
