About 1 minute. The decision: SIM sometimes takes units out of service for training or maintenance. A day-ahead forecast of volume per division could flag days when that is risky. The user is proposed, not confirmed. The target is the daily count of interventions published in the open data, for each of Divisions 1 to 6. Timing matters: the portal publishes about 2 days late, so a forecast made on D minus 1 can only use counts up to D minus 3. Success means a lower MAE than a recent same-weekday average. We forecast recorded volume, not workload.
