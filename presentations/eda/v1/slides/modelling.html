What this means for the model

Build the model

Features

Recent levels ending D−3, weekday, month, holidays. No linear trend: it over-predicts after December 2025. Never incident type or vehicles.

Structure

One pooled model with a division effect, since most movement is shared. Division 6 gets its own recent-level features.

Target and loss

Poisson or Tweedie objective on raw counts, or log1p, chosen on validation MAE and always scored on the original scale.

Judge the model

Split

Rolling-origin validation on 2021-2024, test on 2025 onward. No k-fold cross-validation: it would train on the future.

Baselines

Same-weekday mean, 13 or 26 weeks: MAE 8.16. Plain 28-day mean: MAE 8.03, the harder comparator. We report both.

Evaluation

MAE and bias per division and per month, with and without the 134 anomaly days.
